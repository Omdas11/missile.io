## Originality statement

missile.io is an original work. Its **survival gameplay concept** (a rocket in
constant forward flight that you steer by holding left/right, dodging homing
missiles while collecting pickups against a 2-minute timer) is **inspired by**
the game "2 Minutes in Space" — but **no code, assets, or art were copied
from it or from any other game**. Every line of code in this repository was
written from scratch for this project, and all gameplay tuning, scoring, and
mechanics are original implementations.
